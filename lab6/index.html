RESEARCH LIFE

Research 🔬

Exercise oncology, women's health, and my Honors thesis.

Exercise Oncology Lab

Tuesday + Thursday

I work in the Exercise Oncology Lab and help support cancer survivors through exercise. The goal is to help improve things like strength, physical function, fatigue, quality of life, and confidence.

Cancer Wellness Program

I work with participants through the Cancer Wellness Program at Prisma Health. I help coach participants through strength-training sessions and learn how exercise can become part of cancer care.

Honors Thesis

My thesis focuses on women's health research and education, particularly what women know and experience in everyday life and healthcare.

I'm interested in menstrual health education, misinformation, lived experience, and the relationship between patients, clinicians, and researchers.

Research reminder ✨

Keep asking questions. The best research starts with being genuinely curious about people's experiences.
