01 ABOUT

I like
people.

I'm interested in the things that make people who they are—and how those things shape health.

I'm a senior studying Biological Sciences at the University of South Carolina Honors College. Most of my time has been spent somewhere between research, patient care, school, work, and trying to experience as much of life as possible.

I tend to be curious about the spaces between things: science and lived experience, prevention and treatment, research and the people it is supposed to serve.
