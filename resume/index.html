01 — THE PERSON BEHIND THE PAGE

A little about me.

I've always been interested in what makes people who they are: the experiences they carry, the things they care about, and the little details that make every person different.

I'm currently studying biological sciences at the University of South Carolina, where my interests in research, human health, and community engagement continue to grow.

Outside the classroom, you'll usually find me working, running or cycling, exploring somewhere new, finding something special at a thrift store, cooking for friends, or getting lost in a good conversation.

I'm learning that a meaningful life isn't built around one accomplishment. It's built through showing up, staying open to new perspectives, and finding ways to make a difference in the lives of others.
